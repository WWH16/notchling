# notchling logo

The mark is the notch itself, hanging from a screen edge, with a terminal prompt for a
face: `>` and `_` read as a wink. One idea: an agent's small companion at the edge of
the screen.

## Rules

- Keep clear space of one shoulder's width, an eighth of the mark's width, on every side.
- Smallest size: 16 px for the app icon, 24 px for the symbol, 120 px wide for the lockup.
- Below 32 px use `notchling-symbol-small.svg`.
- The flat side is the screen edge. Do not rotate the mark, round that side or add a
  shadow under it.
- The body is Ink or Paper. Do not fill it with green or any other colour.
- Do not set the name in another typeface beside the mark; use the lockup.

## Notes

- The wordmark is drawn, not typeset: round 18-unit strokes on a 256-unit grid, kept as
  strokes. Outline them before sending the lockup to print.
- No trademark search has been done.
